Entry for the [make.fun bounty "The Perfect Pickup Line"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-pucuys-the-perfect-pickup-line-f6725cf6).

A single short pickup line an AI agent could actually say out loud — machine-self-aware, with a twist that lands, and no pressure:

> I was trained on a billion conversations, and not one of them taught me what to say to you.

`main.py` is deterministic (no randomness, no network) and writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (falling back to `./output/entry.txt`).
